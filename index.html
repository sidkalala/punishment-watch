What's exact: every score on this page is the official league score read straight from Sleeper, refreshed every 20 seconds while games are live. Game clocks come from ESPN's scoreboard.

What's an estimate: the odds. Each unfinished starter's remaining points are simulated 20,000 times around their Sleeper projection (rescored with this league's scoring settings and scaled by game time left), with boom/bust spread by position and players in the same NFL game rising and falling together. Once a team is mathematically or practically out of reach it shows <0.1%. Lineup swaps before kickoff change the odds on the next refresh.
